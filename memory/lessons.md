# ApexMind — Lessons Learned

If-then rules distilled by the **Reflection** role from resolved predictions.
A future Specialist should be able to read any line and apply it directly.

Format: `- [YYYY-MM-DD] (Category) <trigger pattern> → <corrected behaviour>`
The optional `(Category)` tag routes a lesson to matching markets in the briefing;
untagged lessons are general and always shown.

<!-- The Reflection role appends below this line. Prune aggressively: keep the set
     small and sharp. Seed heuristics below are starting priors, not scripture. -->

- [seed] When tempted to deviate >15% from the market on a single headline → stop and find the *mechanism*; headlines are usually already priced.
- [seed] When a market looks like "easy money" → re-read the resolution criteria first; the edge is often an illusion from misreading the deadline or source of truth.
- [seed] When confidence is high but the causal model has <2 independent drivers → lower confidence; thin models are overconfident.
